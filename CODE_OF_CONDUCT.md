# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We expect participants to be respectful, constructive, inclusive, and open to
different viewpoints. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats,
sexualized behavior, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict
participation when behavior violates this standard. They will prioritize the
safety and dignity of participants when handling reports.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers on
GitHub. Do not include sensitive security details in a public issue; use the
process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
